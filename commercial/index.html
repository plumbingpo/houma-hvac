Food Service Comfort

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner and fryer adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air outside all shift long, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without upsetting the building's pressure. Houma HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep both the kitchen and the dining room comfortable, and we plan visits around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical concerns for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with written service records that demonstrate a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work around your business, not the other way around. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and we keep disruption to a minimum so the kitchen keeps running and the dining room stays comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Houma HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

Every hour without climate control costs your business money. In a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. In a retail store, it sends shoppers home on a summer afternoon. Houma HVAC provides fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Houma and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.
